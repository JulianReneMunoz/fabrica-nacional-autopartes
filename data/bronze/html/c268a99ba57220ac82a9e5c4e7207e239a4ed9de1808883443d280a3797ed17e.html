Honda
Motorcycles

#1 of motorcycles in the world

We are the brand of the power of dreams and we accompany Colombians to make real their own dreams.

We are the only domestic production plant, worldwide, licensed to manufacture and assemble Honda motorcycles, by our high quality standards. Our assembly plant is located in Acopi, Yumbo, Valle del Cauca.

We sell 100 cc up to 250 cc, Honda spare parts and accessories.

We import high cilinder Honda motorcycles.

We have our own distribution network and a network of distributors that allows to have coverage in the national territory: 450 points of sale with presence in the 32 departments,  265 Repair shops in the country and 1,265 spare parts warehouses.
