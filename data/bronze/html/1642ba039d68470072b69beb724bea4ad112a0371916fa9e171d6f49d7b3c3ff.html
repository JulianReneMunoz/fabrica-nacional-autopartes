Environmental

We have been manufacturing solid waste collection equipment since 1981, leading the innovation and evolution of garbage compactor boxes and steel containers in Colombia.

We market complementary waste management equipment such as hydraulic arms, side loading boxes, container cleaners and sweepers.

We offer a wide range of compacting boxes designed with state-of-the-art components for longer equipment life and flexible operation in any terrain of demanding Andean topography.

We have a presence in 29 departments nationwide, we have more than 250 national and international clients, located in countries such as Panama, Venezuela, Ecuador, India and Chile.

We are leaders in the implementation of high-strength, anti-wear steels in boxes and have patented technologies to make its collection operation more cost-effective.
