Colombian pride

On May 31, 1958, the National Factory of bodyworks – Fanalca was established between the Losada and Herrera families.

Today, it is consolidated as a diverse, entrepreneurial company and a leader in the sectors in which it has a stake through its business units.

This shows the commitment of our founders, who have continued their legacy and each one of the collaborators who are part of the organization, so that the growth of the company adds value to society and generates an economic, social and environmental balance.
