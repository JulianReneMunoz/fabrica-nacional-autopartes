# Honda

In 1962, Honda went from two to four wheels and **the brand’s first sports car, the S360, was born**, reaching a speed of 100 km per hour.

During 1972, **the renowned Honda Civic was launched**, making a difference in the history of compact cars as it was the first to meet the environmental standards established by the United States.

In Colombia, **Fanalca began importing and marketing Honda vehicles in the 1990s** and is consolidated as the only brand in the country to offer a Total True Guarantee: labor, inputs and spare parts at no cost, for 2 years and without mileage limit. Currently, the Honda Total Guarantee is the only one on the market that includes preventive, corrective and predictive maintenance at no cost.

Agreeing with the brand’s purpose of creating mobility solutions and ensuring their innovation, Honda entered robotics and in 2000 introduced Asimo, the world’s most advanced humanoid robot. In addition, in 2003, launched the Hondajet, making his first flight over North Carolina, United States.
