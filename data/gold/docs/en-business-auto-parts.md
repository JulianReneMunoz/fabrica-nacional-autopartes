# Auto parts

#### We have production plants in Acopi, Yumbo (Valle del Cauca), Bogotá (Cundinamarca) and Medellin (Antioquia).

At our **Yumbo** and **Bogota** plant we manufacture metal parts for cars and commercial vehicles such as trucks, pickups, buses, buses; as well as doors, crossbars and chassis (frames) supports.

In the plant located in **Medellin** is carried out assembly of metal parts for cars and commercial vehicles; such as floor panels, motor cots and rear ends.
