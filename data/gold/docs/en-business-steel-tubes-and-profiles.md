# Steel tubes

We manufacture and market steel products

Welded **steel tubes** in Cold Rolled, Hot Rolled, Structural and Galvanized sheet.

**Open steel profiles** for construction such as: Profile C, Mezzanine Profile, Beams, Steel Deck, Curtain Strap, Drywall Lightweight Drywall System and Steel Framing.

We offer the most complete **portfolio** of steel pipes manufactured with the highest **geometric precision** ensuring product **performance.**

We have production plants in **Acopi, Yumbo** (Valle del Cauca) and **Guachené** (Cauca).
